## Brand & Style
This design system embodies the official civic identity of Kecamatan Loa Kulu within Kabupaten Kutai Kartanegara, Kalimantan Timur. It balances institutional authority with modern public service accessibility, projecting credibility, warmth, and clarity without bureaucratic friction.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate/Modern** civic clarity with **Editorial Heritage** cues:
- **Authority & Heritage:** Deep maritime navy anchors public administration, accented by regal gold inspired by the regional heritage of Kutai Kartanegara.
- **Approachability:** Warm cream canvas bases prevent institutional austerity, offering an inviting, human-centric municipal digital counter.
- **Structure:** Clean, systematic layouts, crisp hairline borders, disciplined typographic contrast, and restrained elevation to ensure high legibility across diverse demographic cohorts, bandwidth limits, and devices.

## Colors
The color scheme is rooted in formal administrative rigor, accented by civic dignity:

- **Primary (`#14304F` - Biru Bahari Tua):** Anchors headers, official navigation, formal document cards, and primary municipal calls-to-action.
- **Secondary (`#C9A227` - Emas Kutai):** Used for structural rules, institutional insignia highlights, active navigation pips, badge borders, and featured service spotlights.
- **Tertiary / Alert (`#9B1C1C` - Merah Pengumuman):** Reserved strictly for critical announcements, urgent flood/weather warnings, disaster alerts, and deadline indicators.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1B2430` - Arang Kontras Tinggi):** Serves as high-contrast body text, headings, and high-legibility UI labels to guarantee WCAG AAA compliance.
- **Neutral Surface / Background (`#F7F5EF` - Krem Hangat):** The general canvas tone, creating a soft, glare-free reading environment superior to harsh sterile white.
- **Surface Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Applied exclusively to functional service cards, data tiles, interactive forms, and dialog surfaces to maintain crisp contrast against the warm cream background.
- **Subtle Border Rule (`rgba(20, 48, 79, 0.12)`):** Defines structured table boundaries, card edges, and municipal dividing lines.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy reflects formal state communication:
- **Headlines (`Source Serif 4`):** Delivers editorial authority, stability, and historical prestige for governmental titles, official releases, and section markers.
- **Body & Data (`Source Sans 3`):** Ensures flawless legibility for long-form bureaucratic regulations, administrative procedures, statistics, and interactive form fields.
- **Kerning & Leading:** Body text utilizes relaxed leading (minimum 1.45–1.5x) to accommodate public readers of all age groups and visual acuity levels.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Tabular numerals (`tnum`) must be enforced on numerical tables, population counts, and budgetary reporting.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined 12-column grid system with a maximum content container width of `1280px`:

- **Breakpoints:** Mobile (`< 768px`), Tablet (`768px - 1024px`), Desktop (`> 1024px`).
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8pt architectural rhythm, with `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) handling standard structural division.
- **Section Pacing:** Major municipal modules (e.g., Quick Services, Wilayah Statistics, Berita Terkini) maintain generous vertical margins (`48px` to `64px`) to establish discrete information zones.
- **Mobile Reflow:** 4-column quick service grids collapse to 2 columns on mobile devices, while data stat tiles stack linearly into dual-column metric cards.

## Elevation & Depth
In line with official governmental aesthetics, depth is communicated through crisp structural boundaries and soft ambient shading rather than dramatic elevation:

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Used for base surfaces (`#F7F5EF`) and static structural panels.
- **Level 1 (Card Default):** `#FFFFFF` cards with a crisp hairline border (`1px solid rgba(20, 48, 79, 0.08)`) and a subtle ambient shadow: `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(20, 48, 79, 0.06), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(20, 48, 79, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Quick Portals):** Activated on hover for portal service links: `0 8px 18px -3px rgba(20, 48, 79, 0.10), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(20, 48, 79, 0.04)`, accompanied by an accent border shift to `#C9A227`.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Emergency Banner Sticky):** `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(20, 48, 79, 0.16)`.
- **Rule of Thumb:** Shadows are never neutral grey; they carry a slight navy tint (`#14304F` at low opacities) to ground elements into the municipal palette.

## Shapes
A conservative, structural roundedness (`roundedness: 1` — Soft, base `4px`, `rounded-lg: 8px`) is applied across all components:

- **Institutional Stance:** Sharp enough to appear authoritative, structured, and formal, avoiding playful bulbous geometry while staying modern and accessible.
- **Corner Radii:** Buttons, input fields, and tags use `4px` to `6px`. Container cards, portal tiles, and image thumbnails use `8px`.
- **Pills (`9999px`):** Restricted solely to status indicators (e.g., "Aktif", "Selesai", "Kategori Berita").

## Components

### 1. Top Ribbon & Institutional Header
- **Top Ribbon (Pita Kontak & Jam Layanan):** Sits at `#14304F` dark surface with text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and accents in `#C9A227`. Displays operating hours (Senin - Jumat 08:00 - 16:00 WITA), hotline/WhatsApp pengaduan, and social links.
- **Main Nav Header:** Pure white background or `#F7F5EF` with 2px bottom border in `#C9A227`. Houses the official Lambang Daerah Kutai Kartanegara, title ("Kecamatan Loa Kulu"), and clean 8-item navigation (Beranda, Profil, Pemerintahan, Layanan Publik, PPID, Data, Publikasi, Kontak) with gold active underlines.

### 2. Emergency / Important Notice Banner (Pengumuman Darurat)
- **Visuals:** High-visibility banner featuring `#9B1C1C` border and light tinted fill (`rgba(155, 28, 28, 0.06)`).
- **Content:** Bold label `[PENGUMUMAN PENTING]`, concise notification text, date stamp, and direct call-to-action button with high contrast.

### 3. Quick Service Portal Cards (Portal Layanan Cepat)
- **Structure:** Clean white cards (`#FFFFFF`) with `8px` corner radius, centered icon enclosed in a soft navy circle (`rgba(20, 48, 79, 0.06)`), title in `Source Serif 4`, and brief operational description.
- **Hover State:** Micro-elevation to Level 2 with a top border accent line (`3px solid #C9A227`).

### 4. Interactive Regional Statistics (Statistik Wilayah)
- **Structure:** Modular numeric data cards displaying key demographic indicators (Jumlah Penduduk, Luas Wilayah, Jumlah Desa/Kelurahan, Layanan Terselesaikan).
- **Typographic Treatment:** Large bold tabular numbers in `#14304F`, small descriptive subtitle in `#1B2430`, and a gold trend badge.

### 5. News & Agenda Cards (Berita & Agenda Kecamatan)
- **News Item:** High-aspect 16:9 thumbnail, gold category badge (`Pemerintahan`, `Pembangunan`, `Sosial`), publication date, serif title, and summary excerpt.
- **Agenda Block:** Prominent date tile with blue background and white day/month numerals, paired with event name, location, and coordinator notes.

### 6. Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#14304F` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` border radius, hover state shifting to lighter navy (`#1E436D`).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with `1.5px solid #14304F` border, navy text, hover state filling with `#F7F5EF`.
- **Accent Action:** `#C9A227` background, `#1B2430` text for prominent public portals (e.g., "Lapor Camat").

### 7. Form Controls & Inputs
- **Base Input:** White surface, `1px solid rgba(20, 48, 79, 0.2)` border, `4px` radius, `12px 16px` padding.
- **Focus State:** `2px solid #14304F` with an outer gold glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(201, 162, 39, 0.25)`).

### 8. Institutional Footer
- **Structure:** Full-bleed `#14304F` surface with `#C9A227` horizontal dividing accents.
- **Content Columns:** Official regional insignia, legal address, contact center, jurisdictional links (Pemkab Kukar, Disdukcapil, Bappeda), and statutory copyright declaration conforming to Indonesian local government web standards.
